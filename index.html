Understand Your Drinking Risk

Three questions. A clearer picture of your drinking.

Answer three short questions about your drinking habits to get an AUDIT-C screening score and understand whether your drinking pattern may put your health at risk.

How it works

  1. 1
    Answer 3 questions

    Tell us how often you drink, how much you typically drink, and how often you drink heavily.

  2. 2
    See your score

    Get a quick indication of your drinking risk based on your answers.

  3. 3
    Decide your next step

    Whether you want to cut back or quit, start making changes one day at a time.

For people who feel stuck with alcohol and want a self-driven way back.

10Drinks is for anyone who wants to cut back or stop — whether you drink most days, binge on weekends, or have already tried to quit and slipped. You do not need a perfect first week. You need a score you can improve.

You want control, not a spectacle

No leaderboards. No public streak shame. Your Best Score and your last Journey stay with you.

You can be honest about drinking days

Logging a drinking day is not failure. It uses one of ten. Honesty is the win — a real score is one you can actually beat.

You care about the next match

One hard day does not erase the Journey. Trajectory matters more than any single day.

Willpower is easier when the goal is personal and finite.

Open-ended “never drink again” can feel too large. A Journey is bounded: 10 drinking days, then you close the match and start again. You always know the score you are trying to beat — because you built it.

A previous self-built score

Your last Journey is the benchmark. Improvement is counted in sober days against the same 10 drinking days, so progress is readable: 7, then 10, then 13.

Logging honestly is the win

Each journey is a fresh chance to beat your Best Score. A real score is one you can actually beat. Logging a drinking day uses one of ten. It does not wipe the Journey.

Five ways a Journey can be a win

You win here in five ways. Any one of these is a victory. You don’t have to quit. You just have to be slightly better than yesterday.

  • Add one more sober day than last time.
  • Don’t increase your intake.
  • Lower your AUDIT-C score by even one point.
  • Beat your personal best.
  • Reach a milestone you haven’t hit before.

Your log stays on this device.

No accounts. The trial is 15 wall-clock days from the first time you open the app, not the Day count on the dashboard.

On this device

  • Journey data stays in your browser storage. 10Drinks does not run accounts or upload your log to a server.
  • Drink-risk (AUDIT-C) answers are kept for this session only.
  • Export progress saves a backup file you control. Import progress restores from that file.
  • Reset all data clears journeys, streaks, and history on this device only. The app asks you to type RESET before it erases anything.

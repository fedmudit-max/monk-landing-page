Frequently Asked Questions

How does 10Drinks work?

Every day, you answer one simple question: Did you drink alcohol today?

10Drinks tracks your alcohol-free days and drinking days through personal challenges called Journeys. Each Journey ends after 10 drinking days.

Your goal is to accumulate as many alcohol-free days as possible. For example, a score of 30–5 means 30 alcohol-free days and 5 drinking days.

What happens if I drink again? Will I lose my progress?

No. Your alcohol-free days are never erased.

Think of football. Conceding a goal doesn't mean you've lost the match. Even losing a match doesn't necessarily mean you've lost the World Cup.

The same applies to your relationship with alcohol. One drinking day doesn't define your entire Journey. Your previous achievements remain, and tomorrow is another opportunity to move forward.

Don't judge your entire journey by a single day.

Why does a Journey end after 10 drinking days?

Imagine you're playing football against Brazil.

In your first match, you lose 0–10. In the next match, you manage to score once and finish 1–10.

You still lost, but something changed. You scored a goal against a team you couldn't score against before. That's progress.

Now imagine Brazil can never score more than 10 goals against you, but there's no limit to how many you can score.

That's how 10Drinks measures your Journeys.

Every alcohol-free day is a goal for you. Every drinking day is a goal against you. Reaching 10 drinking days ends that Journey, allowing you to compare your progress with the next one.

Your challenge is to improve on your previous score while aiming to remain alcohol-free for as long as possible.

You don't have to beat Brazil today. Just aim to do better than last time.

And if you never reach 10 drinking days, your Journey continues indefinitely.

Does 10Drinks encourage me to drink 10 times?

Absolutely not. The 10 drinking days are a measurement, not an allowance or a target.

The purpose of 10Drinks is to help you reduce or quit drinking. Ideally, you would remain alcohol-free and never reach the 10-drinking-day limit.

The limit simply provides a consistent way to measure your drinking patterns and compare progress between Journeys.

And if you do drink, remember: Drinking is an event, not your identity. One decision doesn't make you a failure or erase your ability to change.

Use that experience as feedback, not as a reason to give up. What matters is the choices you make moving forward.

After the trial, you can continue tracking for free or subscribe to Premium for advanced features.

No automatic charges. A paid subscription starts only when you choose to purchase one through Google Play.

What if I forget to log a day?

You can record missed days later using the app's catch-up process.

Daily reminders can also help you maintain a consistent tracking habit.

Can 10Drinks actually help me reduce or quit drinking?

10Drinks is designed to encourage awareness, consistency, and gradual improvement through daily tracking and personal challenges.

It can support your efforts to change your drinking habits, but it isn't a medical treatment or a guaranteed solution for alcohol dependence.

If you may be physically dependent on alcohol, consult a healthcare professional before stopping abruptly, as withdrawal can be dangerous.

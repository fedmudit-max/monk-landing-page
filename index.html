How It Works

  1. Every Day Has Two Choices

    ✓ I Did Not Drink Today Adds 1 sober day to score. 10 I Drank Today Uses 1 of the 10 drinking days.

  2. Build Your Sober Score Against 10 Drinking Days

  3. Step 3 — Beat your score

    When all 10 drinking days are used, the journey ends. Your score is your sober days. The next journey starts with one goal: beat it.

Two Goals. One App.

Built for people who want to drink less — or quit.

📉

I want to drink less.

Cut back without committing to quitting.

  • Track sober days against 10 drinking days per Journey.
  • Set a focus on drinking frequency, quantity, or heavy occasions.
  • Use the AUDIT-C meter to understand your reported drinking pattern.

The goal: more sober days and a clearer picture of your drinking.

🚫

I want to quit drinking.

You don't have to start with a perfect streak.

  • Log sober days and drinking days honestly.
  • Aim for more sober days than in your previous Journey.
  • Keep improving until you're ready to leave drinking behind.
  • Use the AUDIT-C meter to understand your reported drinking pattern.

The goal: make progress toward quitting, one Journey at a time.

You don’t have to decide today. Start logging. The journey works either way.
